## AutoCAD / CAD and engineering design software
CAD software benefits from a workstation-class or at least a capable discrete GPU with drivers certified/optimized
for the specific application (ISV certification — check the software vendor's certified hardware list, since gaming
GPUs and workstation GPUs can behave differently under CAD workloads even at similar raw specs). RAM
requirements scale with model/assembly complexity: 16GB is a baseline for coursework-scale drawings, 32GB+
for large assemblies or simulation work. A precise pointing device (a proper mouse, not just the trackpad) is
close to a hard requirement for comfortable CAD work — see accessory guidance. A colour-accurate, reasonably
large display helps but matters less than GPU driver certification and RAM headroom.
