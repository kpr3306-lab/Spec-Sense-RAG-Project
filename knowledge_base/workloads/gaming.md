## Gaming
Frame rate at a given resolution/settings is primarily determined by the discrete GPU model and its configured
TGP (see hardware glossary), then VRAM capacity for higher resolutions/texture settings, then CPU (especially for
CPU-bound titles and high frame-rate esports play). A high-refresh-rate display (120Hz+) is only useful if the GPU
can actually sustain frame rates near that refresh rate at the player's preferred settings — pairing a high-refresh
panel with a modest GPU means the panel's extra refresh headroom mostly goes unused. Sustained performance
(cooling design, not just peak GPU spec) matters a lot for longer gaming sessions, since thermal throttling can
significantly cut frame rates after the first 15-20 minutes under load. 16GB RAM is a reasonable baseline for
current titles; some newer/AAA titles benefit from 32GB.
