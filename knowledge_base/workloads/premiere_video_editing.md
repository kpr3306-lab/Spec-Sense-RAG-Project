## Premiere Pro / video editing
Video editing performance depends on CPU (for effects/encoding), GPU (for accelerated effects, colour grading,
and export via hardware encoders), RAM (for timeline scrubbing and multiple open projects), and fast storage
(for reading high-bitrate footage smoothly). Recommended baseline for 1080p-4K editing: 16-32GB RAM, a discrete
GPU with at least 6-8GB VRAM for 4K work, and an NVMe SSD (ideally with a second drive/slot for a dedicated
scratch disk and footage storage). A colour-accurate, high-brightness display (wide gamut coverage) is a strong
soft-preference for anyone doing colour grading professionally.
